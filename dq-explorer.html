Notes on each stage the full explanations are in the modules

Stacks module 01

A near and a far partial stack of the same line, zero phase and in relative amplitude. The near stack approximates the intercept and far minus near is proportional to the gradient. Polarity is decided once for the whole line: if the sand top on the middle trace is a peak, both stacks are flipped so the reservoir contrast reads as a trough.

Picks module 02

The near stack is reduced to its zero crossings and, between each pair of crossings, its single largest peak or trough. Samples below one percent of the largest amplitude on the trace do not count. The far stack is never picked; it is read at the near-stack picks.

Quadrant numbers module 03

Every sample between two picks gets a number from 1 to 9 for where it lies in its loop: 1 a trough, 9 a peak, 5 the zero crossing, 2 to 4 after an extremum and 6 to 8 before the next one.

Enhanced traces module 04

Each quadrant number names a phase rotation from the published table. Each sample of the near stack is read from its rotated copy, which raises it to the equivalent of a peak or trough; the result is QN. The far stack read the same way, at the near-stack numbers, gives QF. The table applies as printed on rising and falling limbs alike.

DQ module 05

The distance of the point (QN, QF − QN) from the origin of the crossplot, by Pythagoras. Peaks, troughs and samples on rising limbs are positive and the rest negative. The section uses the published DQ color bar, seven bands each side of zero.

Theta PX module 07

The angle of the same point on the rotated, split crossplot, folded into 0 to 90 degrees and given the sign of the DQ. The distance reports how strong the response is and the angle which way it points. Where the distance is small the angle is poorly determined and swings.

RDQ crossplot modules 06 and 09

The crossplot rotated by 16 degrees so the background trend lies along the x axis, and the samples numbered 3 to 7 reflected in it. A rotation about the origin leaves every distance unchanged; it makes the vertical coordinate a departure from the background. Clusters picked on this plot are found on the section by dragging a box.

Model

Synthetic throughout: quartz sand with Gassmann fluid substitution, Shuey’s two terms, a Ricker wavelet, 0–10° near and 20–30° far stacks. No field data.
